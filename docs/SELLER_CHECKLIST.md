# Malrocut Seller Checklist

Use this checklist before selling or delivering Malrocut beta setup support on Kmong, Soomgo, or similar marketplaces.

## Sales readiness checklist

- [ ] Demo video prepared.
- [ ] Screenshot prepared.
- [ ] Installation support policy written.
- [ ] Refund policy written.
- [ ] Beta disclaimer included in the listing.
- [ ] Required customer environment listed.
- [ ] Known limitations listed.
- [ ] Delivery files or repository access prepared.
- [ ] Support contact and response time prepared.

## Required customer environment

- Windows 10 or Windows 11.
- Python 3.10+ installed and available in PATH.
- Node.js LTS and npm installed and available in PATH.
- FFmpeg and ffprobe installed and available in PATH.
- Enough disk space for source videos, rendered videos, thumbnails, subtitles, and ZIP files.

## Known limitations

- Beta software; rendering and prompt behavior may change.
- Best with short videos during beta testing.
- No payment, license server, cloud queue, CapCut export, or Whisper speech recognition included in this package.
- FFmpeg must be installed separately by the customer or included in a supported setup service.

## Installation support policy

State exactly what is included, such as remote installation help, environment check, first startup, and one sample render. Also state what is not included, such as custom feature development, customer PC repair, or unsupported third-party software troubleshooting.

## Refund policy

Recommended policy: refunds are available only before installation/support work begins, or when Malrocut cannot be started on a customer PC that meets the stated environment requirements. Adjust this policy for marketplace rules and local law.

## Beta disclaimer

Malrocut 0.1.0-beta is early software. Customers should test with copied files, keep originals backed up, and verify all output before commercial use.
